Subject: [NOTICE] CODE RED — Effective January 3, 2027

Big Bitcoin®
CODE RED
Notice of Color Transition
Effective January 3, 2027

To the Bitcoin Mailing List,

This letter serves as formal notice that Big Bitcoin® has made the decision to change Bitcoin's color from orange to red, effective January 3, 2027.

After careful internal consideration, it has been determined that red better represents Bitcoin's direction moving forward.

This is a notice, not a proposal.

For the avoidance of doubt, this announcement is not a Bitcoin Improvement Proposal (BIP), a request for comments, or an invitation to establish consensus. No community vote, signaling period, or governance process is required. This notice is provided as a courtesy. We control the narrative.

Effective January 3, the following changes will take effect:

1. Red will replace orange as Bitcoin's color.

2. All Bitcoin-related branding, logos, and visual materials should transition to red (RGB: (255, 0, 0), CMYK: (0, 100, 100, 0), HSL: (0°, 100%, 50%), HEX: #FF0000)

3. Orange will be considered a legacy color and phased out accordingly to remain in compliance with Big Bitcoin® branding standards.

4. The Bitcoin protocol, monetary policy, and consensus rules will remain unchanged.

We recognize orange's longstanding association with Bitcoin and appreciate its contributions over the past 18 years. However, after extensive consideration, Big Bitcoin® believes the time has come for a new chapter.

We understand that some members of the community may disagree with this decision. While feedback is appreciated, the decision is final.

No further action or approval is required.

Thank you for your continued participation in Bitcoin.

Sincerely,

Big Bitcoin®
In control.
